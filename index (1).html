ATTENDING

with Lara Kruger & Elzahn Nel

Prince Alfred Hamlet

19 – 21 June 2026

Two days of genuine attention can sometimes unlock what months of busyness hasn't.

The Invitation

This retreat is about paying close attention to what is already there: in the place, in the materials, in your own practice.

You will leave with something to take back into your studio at home, even if you can't name it yet when you arrive.

We will work in and around Elzahn Nel's beautiful studio in the Witzenberg Valley, about 2 hours from Cape Town. The surrounding farmland, garden, and winter light of the Boland interior are both the backdrop and the material.

Who This Is For

Artists working in any medium, at any stage of their practice.

What matters is that you are invested in your work and willing to show up and engage with the place, with the process, and with a small group of fellow artists.

This is not a beginner's introduction, and it is not an advanced masterclass. It is a working retreat.

Programme

The weekend is structured around three guided sessions, with time for independent work and exploration in between.

Friday afternoon

We begin together. An opening session that settles the group into the place and into the mode of working we will carry through the weekend.

Saturday

A full day. A guided session in the morning, independent studio and garden time in the afternoon with optional informal 1:1 consultation with Lara, and a group reflection activity to close the day.

Sunday morning

The final session focuses on what has emerged and how to carry it forward into your practice. We end around midday.

Your Facilitators

Lara Kruger and Elzahn Nel are artist friends and long-time creative confidants, excited to be facilitating together for the first time. Their approach is process-led, rooted in observation, intuitive making, and meeting each artist where they are.

Lara Kruger

Lara is a visual artist, facilitator, and the founder of LK Artist Mentorship based in Paarl, where she also hosts monthly studio workshops. She holds a Master's degree in Fine Art, has taught art and lectured in design theory and research, and continues to exhibit and develop her own practice. Her facilitation is process-led and responsive: she works by slowing down, paying close attention, and supporting what is ready to emerge in the work and in the artist making it.

Elzahn Nel

Elzahn is an artist and illustrator whose practice moves fluidly between daily drawing, textiles, installation, and storytelling. Raised between the Boland and the Karoo and shaped by seven years living in Asia, her work is deeply informed by observation, travel, and the transformative potential of creative spaces. Elzahn will bring a guest facilitation session rooted in her own practice and her intimate working relationship with this specific place.
